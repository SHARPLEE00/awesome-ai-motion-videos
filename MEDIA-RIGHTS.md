# Media rights and attribution

Every video, prompt, quoted post, piece of music, brand and depicted asset linked from this repository belongs to its creator or rights holder. Listing it here does not transfer ownership or make it MIT-licensed or free to reuse.

What this repository does and doesn't do:

- **Links, doesn't copy.** Each entry links to the creator's original post on X. Thumbnails are loaded from X's own image servers. No video file is downloaded or re-hosted here.
- **Credits every entry** to the account that posted it.
- **Quotes prompts only when the creator published them**, with a link to where they were posted. Entries without a published prompt have none; we never reconstruct or guess one.
- **Model credits follow the creator's own words.** We don't have access to anyone's generation logs.

The MIT license covers this repository's own scripts, data structure and README text only.

## Corrections and removals

Creators can ask for a credit fix or for their entry to be removed, no questions asked. [Open an issue](https://github.com/SHARPLEE00/awesome-ai-motion-videos/issues/new) with the post link and what you'd like changed, or reply to the maintainer on X. Removals are made within a few days.
